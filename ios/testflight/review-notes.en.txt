Mondrop is the companion app of SoundReel, a private music journal that one family (three people) runs on its own server. The app talks only to that server. It is not open to the public: no public sign-up, no ads, no purchases. The app is in Italian.

The server is private, so the app needs a personal key (entered in the Settings tab) issued by our server; without it there is no library to show. If the review team wants to see the app with data, write to the contact email of this review information and we will send a temporary key.

Main features
1. Share: the Share sheet extension "Mondrop" (and the Share tab) sends a link to the family server; the outcome comes back in the Inbox ("Posta") tab.
2. Home and Search: search the family music library by song, artist or album; resume where you were; new songs and playlists; "Tutte" on the Home opens the list of all playlists.
3. Player: queue, mini player, cover art and lyrics; the queue is kept between launches and never starts playing by itself. From version 1.8.0 there is also a "like" heart and a repeat mode, available from the lock screen and from Bluetooth or car controls.
4. Playlists: the user's own and automatic ones, with search and sort by date. Each song shows whether it is on the phone, online or in error. The list of playlists and the songs of each playlist can be sorted from a menu (by state, name or date; they start with the songs on the phone first). The page of a playlist shows its list at once (it is kept on the phone), with three icon buttons on one line: Play, Shuffle and "Scarica tutta" (download all). From 1.8.0, when the user plays a playlist, its missing songs are downloaded in the background for offline listening, in the order in which they are about to play (a warning appears above a size limit); Settings > Sync has a button, "Controlla le playlist sul telefono", that checks the playlists the phone holds and downloads what is missing.

Please look at
a. Share a link from Safari to Mondrop and check that the outcome appears in the Inbox tab.
b. Play a song from Search and open the full-screen player (cover, lyrics, queue); from 1.8.0, tap the heart.
c. Play a playlist, wait for its downloads and play it again with the phone in airplane mode.
d. In Settings > Sync, tap "Controlla le playlist sul telefono" and read the result. The mini player appears only while something plays.
e. On the page of a playlist, open the sort menu (top right) and choose another order; tap "Scarica tutta" to download what is missing.
